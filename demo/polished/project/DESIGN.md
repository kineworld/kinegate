# KineGate demo visual identity

## Style Prompt
Quiet, precise product cinematography in KineGate's existing yellow and near-black palette. The recorded browser remains the subject. Slowly approach the active control or resulting evidence; use a restrained yellow sweep when the narrative moves from fixture policy to recorded external evidence. Keep provenance and captions outside the camera transform.

## Colors
- `#0b0e11`: background, matching the actual product.
- `#181a20`: caption and context panels.
- `#eaecef`: primary text.
- `#b9bdc6`: secondary text with stronger video contrast.
- `#f0b90b`: KineGate yellow, focus accents and chapter progress.

## Typography
Use Segoe UI for spoken captions and statements: a familiar humanist reading voice available on this Windows render host. Use Consolas for provenance and timing. Product pixels retain their original fonts. Headlines are 60 px when used; captions 30 px, provenance 22 px. The persistent KineGate wordmark is a compact brand label, not a headline.

## Motion
Camera approaches take 1.2–1.7 seconds with sine.inOut; holds are long enough to read. Crossfades last 0.45 seconds. A single yellow wipe marks the evidence chapter. No bounce, shake, flash or decorative overshoot. Captions and provenance remain fixed.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not hide, falsify or re-label actual FIXTURE, REPLAY and SIMULATION provenance.
- Do not replace product pixels with invented UI, fabricated results or successful-trade claims.
- Do not move subtitles with the camera or put captions over the active result.
- Do not use gradients, glitch effects or dense decorative overlays.
- Do not use copyright-unknown samples or purchased generation services.
